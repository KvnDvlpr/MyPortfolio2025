Engineer by degree. Developer in the making.

I'm Kevin Acebuche, an Electronics Engineering graduate from Teresa, Rizal. I'm not an AI automation specialist yet, and I won't pretend to be one. I'm an engineer working my way into the IT industry, aiming to become a developer, and this page is the honest record of that journey so far.

It started with my thesis: an AI-based eggplant leaf disease detector built on Roboflow and a Raspberry Pi. That was the first time I saw code solve a real problem, and I wanted more of it. After graduating in 2025 I took an engineering internship, then trained in CRM and workflow automation with GoHighLevel and ActiveCampaign, and spent six months as a Technical Support Representative on a medical healthcare account, diagnosing issues, finding root causes and documenting every fix.

On my own time I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SQL, then picked up Claude Code as a pair programmer and started shipping: Daywell, a full-stack planner, Exalting AGHAM, and this site. I read every line, test what I build, and own the result.

What I bring today is a troubleshooter's patience, a habit of documenting as I go, and a real appetite to learn. Give me a problem and a codebase, and I'll show up every day until it works.
