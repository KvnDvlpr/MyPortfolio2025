Daywell — daily planner

2025 · Live

daywell-project.vercel.app · Solo project, design to deployment

A planner for managing work, studies, and one's walk with God. Login and signup, tasks that repeat on their own, a focus countdown timer, a calendar with events, a verse of the day, and daily progress.

  • Designed the Postgres schema, migrations, triggers and row-level security so each user only sees their own data.
